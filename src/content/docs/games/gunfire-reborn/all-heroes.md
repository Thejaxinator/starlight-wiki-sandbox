## Crown Prince — 太子 (Taizi) — Spirit Alchemist
- **Stats:** HP `65 -> 90` • Defense `65 -> 110 Shield` • Recovery `10%/s (3.0s delay)` • Move Speed `5.0` ([Source](https://gunfirereborn.fandom.com/wiki/Crown_Prince))
- **Primary Skill (`E`):** Energy Orb — Freezes targets and locks movement; pairs with Energy Echo & Splinter Orb.
- **Secondary Skill (`Q`):** Smoke Grenade — AoE Corrosion cloud with 100% Decay chance for instant Combustion or Miasma fusions.
- **Core Mechanic:** Innate Elemental Synergy — Guaranteed Decay grenades enable effortless Combustion (Fire) or Miasma (Lightning).
- **Hero Talents:** Efficient Orb: Energy Orb CD -2s; Mystic Guard: Max Shield +20; Large Area Smoke: Smoke Grenade radius +40%; Energy Charge: Recover 5% Shield per enemy hit by Energy Orb; Contingency Plan: Per 10% Shield lost, +5% recharge speed & -5% delay
- **Ascension Trees:**
  - Element (Dominance): Pyro/Electro/Acidominance (+40/80/120% elem DMG), Elemental Rage (+30/60/120% DMG vs status), Wildfire (+40/50/60% RoF)
  - Smoke (Hex): Hex Smoke (-40/-50/-60% enemy DMG, +20/40/60% DMG taken), Toxic Smoke (+300/400/500% base DMG), Backpack Expansion
  - Orb (Arcane): Energy Blade (+300/400/500% next weapon hit), Chained Duration (+2/4/6s freeze), Blinding Sphere, Power Source
- **Spiritual Blessings:** Splinter Orb (triple orb + -1s CD/hit), Smogbound Zone (+40% smoke range, +100% status chance), Elemental Maestro (ignores -25% counter, +60/+90% multi-elem DMG), Elemental Orb (auto orbs on weapon hit)
