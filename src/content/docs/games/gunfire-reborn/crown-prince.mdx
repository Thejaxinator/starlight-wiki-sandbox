**Crown Prince** specializes in elemental status application, fusion reactions, and crowd control. **Smoke Grenade** reliably applies **Decay** in an area, letting any Fire or Lightning weapon immediately trigger **Combustion** or **Miasma**, while **Energy Orb** locks targets in place.
