Every hero in **Gunfire Reborn** has distinct base Health, Shield or Armor pools, Movement Speed, a Primary Skill, a Secondary Skill, 5 Hero Talents, and 3 Ascension trees.

## Base Game Heroes (6)

| Hero | Unlock Requirement | Defense Pool | Core Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Crown Prince](../crown-prince/)** | Unlocked by default | Shield | Elemental fusions, Smoke Grenade Decay, Energy Orb freeze |
| **Ao Bai** | Talent Level 25 | Armor | Dual-wielding firearms and explosive damage |
| **Qing Yan** | Talent Level 40 | Armor | Close-quarters shotgun/melee Leap and Cleave |
| **Lei Luo** | Talent Level 55 | Shield | Sniper crits, Chain Lightning, and Fatal Current |
| **Tao** | 400 Soul Essence | Shield | High fire-rate Sword Out stacks and Fatal Bloom |
| **Qian Sui** | 600 Soul Essence | Shield | Tidal Aspis barrier defense and close-range strikes |
