**Gunfire Reborn** blends first-person shooter gunplay with roguelite run progression and hero-based RPG builds, playable solo or in 4-player online co-op.

## Outdated Fandom Corrections

The legacy [Fandom main page](https://gunfirereborn.fandom.com/wiki/Gunfire_Reborn) contradicts itself between 12 characters, 58 weapons, and 65 weapons while omitting recent DLC and seasonal systems. Current verified totals:

- **Playable Heroes:** 14 live heroes (6 base + 8 across four DLCs), with **Lang Yao** and **Loong** announced in the [Lunar Melody DLC](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5301730/Gunfire_Reborn_Lunar_Melody/).
- **Weapons:** 70+ weapons across 9 weapon categories, plus 4 more (**Firefly**, **Red Prime**, **Wolfbrush**, and **Meltpaint**) arriving in [Lunar Melody](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5301730/Gunfire_Reborn_Lunar_Melody/).
- **Difficulty Tiers:** Normal, Elite, Nightmare, and Reincarnation Levels 1 through 10 (Spiritual Blessings and enhanced weapons unlock in Reincarnation).

## Main Acts & Realms

| Stage | Region / Realm | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Act 1** | Longling Tomb | Starting dungeon; weapon drops scale from Lv 1 to Lv 2 |
| **Act 2** | Anxi Desert | Unlocks Gemini inscription etching at the Craftsman |
| **Act 3** | Duo Fjord | Weapons begin dropping with natural Gemini inscriptions |
| **Act 4 / Alt** | Hyperborean Jokul / Twilight Grove / Crystal Vale | Late-run realms and expanded seasonal routes ([Steam Announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1217060/announcements/)) |
