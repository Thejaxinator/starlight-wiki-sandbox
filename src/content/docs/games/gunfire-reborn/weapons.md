Weapons drop with randomized **Inscriptions** and scale in base level by stage. Boss drops always roll using the **next stage's** level and inscription count.

## Stage Drop & Gemini Scaling

| Stage | Drop Level | Inscriptions | Rolls Natural Gemini? | Can Etch Gemini? |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Arms Support Starter Chest** | 0 | 1 | No | No |
| **Longling Tomb 1–2** | 1 | 2 | No | No |
| **Longling Tomb 3** | 2 | 2 | No | No |
| **Longling Tomb 4 & Boss** | 2 | 3 | No | No |
| **Anxi Desert 1** | 3 | 4 | No | Yes |
| **Anxi Desert 2–3 & Boss** | 4 | 4 | No | Yes |
| **Duo Fjord 1** | 5 | 5 | Yes | Yes |
| **Duo Fjord 2–3 & Boss** | 6 | 5 | Yes | Yes |
| **Hyperborean Jokul 1–2 & Boss** | 10 | 5 | Yes | Yes |

## Weapon Categories

- **Rifles (Normal Ammo):** Big Hippo, Cavalry, Crimson Firescale, Dragonchaser, Hexagon, Lightning Blast, Rainbow Arch, Revealer, Tracker.
- **Submachine Guns (Normal Ammo):** Angelic Aura, Concealed Ammo, Demonlore, Dual Fang, Scalpel, Star Devourer, Wolf Gaze.
- **Pistols (Mostly Large Ammo):** Arc Light, Aura of Venom, Cloud Weaver *(Normal)*, Foundry *(Unlimited)*, Glimmering *(reworked with Weapon Skill in S8)*, Icy Spear, Prism *(reworked with tracking mark & Weapon Skill in S8)*, Scorching Rounds, Star Ring *(Normal)*, Sunder, Talisman, Viper Sight.
- **Shotguns (Large / Special Ammo):** Argus, Hell *(Special)*, Illusion, Porcupine, Pupil, Wheel Saw, Wild Hunt *(Special)*.
- **Snipers (Mostly Special Ammo):** Bloody Drill, Brick, Double Caliber, Golden Bow, Goshawk, Lightning Ksana *(Shield rework in S8)*, Piercing Flame, Sting *(Large)*, Strike Wing, Woodpecker *(Large)*.
- **Launchers (Special / Large Ammo):** Bone Dragon, Deafening Mortar, Dragon Breath *(Quick Reaction instant-reload rework in S8)*, Frenzied Shark, Justice, Shrieker, Tempest, Thunder Storm, Tiger Cannon.
- **Injectors (Normal / Large Ammo):** Clawspray, Fire Dragon, Jet Octopus *(Large)*, Laser Gloves, Radioactive Gauntlet, Rainbow, Thunderclap Gloves.
- **Melee Weapons:** Fire Tower, Flowing Light, Poisonous Ghost, Storm Chaser.
- **Staves & Recent DLC Additions:** Crane Chant, Phoenix Roar, Starfly; plus [Echoes from Primal Grove](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1217060/announcements/detail/528735466048978950) additions (**Armillary Sash**, **Drift Shard**, **Jackpot**, **Viper Sight**) and announced [Lunar Melody](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5301730/Gunfire_Reborn_Lunar_Melody/) weapons (**Firefly**, **Red Prime**, **Wolfbrush**, **Meltpaint**).
